# StudyTrack

**A personal study tracker built for focused, consistent learning.**

🔗 **Live app → [gagan-meena.github.io/StudyTrack](https://gagan-meena.github.io/StudyTrack)**

---

## What is StudyTrack?

StudyTrack is a free, no-login study companion that helps you track how much time you spend studying, stay consistent with a streak system, and plan what to study each day all from your browser with no account required.

Your data lives entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

---

## Features

### 📊 Dashboard
See everything at a glance today's study time, all-time hours, your current streak, and activity heatmap showing your study history over the last 53 weeks. Set a daily hour goal and watch the progress bar fill up as you study.

### 📚 Subjects
Add subjects and give each one a color. Inside each subject, maintain a topic checklist tick off topics as you study them and track how far through the syllabus you are. Each subject shows a progress bar based on completed topics.

### 📈 Analytics
See weekly bar charts of your study hours either as a stacked overview of all subjects combined or broken down per subject. Navigate back through previous weeks and set per-subject daily goals.

### 🍅 Pomodoro Timer
A built-in focus timer with customizable focus, short break, and long break durations. Select a subject before starting when the timer ends the session is automatically logged to that subject. Every 4 focus sessions triggers a long break.

### ✅ Side Tasks
A lightweight task list for anything that doesn't belong to a specific subject errands, assignments, reminders. Set priority (High / Medium / Low) and mark tasks done when complete.

### 📋 Activity Log
A full timestamped log of everything sessions completed, subjects added, tasks created. Export all your data as a JSON file anytime, and import it back if you switch devices or browsers.

---

## Privacy

- ✅ No account, no login, no sign-up
- ✅ All data stored locally in your browser (localStorage)
- ✅ Nothing is sent to any server ever
- ✅ Export your data anytime as a JSON backup
